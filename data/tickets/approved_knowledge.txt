ACME Support Services - Approved Customer Support Knowledge

PAYMENT ISSUE:
If payment is deducted but the order status remains unpaid, the customer should wait up to 30 minutes for reconciliation.
If the status does not update after 30 minutes, support should verify the transaction and escalate the issue.

REFUND POLICY:
Refund information must only be provided when it is available in the approved knowledge base.
Do not promise a refund without approved information.

GENERAL RULE:
Support agents must not invent policies, timelines, refunds, compensation, or actions that are not present in the approved knowledge base.
If the required information is unavailable, the response should clearly state that the information is not available.
